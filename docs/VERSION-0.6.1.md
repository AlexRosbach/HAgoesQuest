# Version 0.6.1 – More readable menu and adjustable watch

The menu now uses brighter, fully opaque slate-blue surfaces, white primary text, and clearly separated headings and descriptions. Text colors on normal, primary, and hint surfaces achieve a calculated contrast of at least 4.5:1. Actual headset readability under different passthrough lighting remains part of device acceptance.

The four startup tiles have more detailed original 3D objects: a lightbulb with a screw base, a furnished floor plan, a connection hub, and a gear. Ceramic and brushed-metal textures are generated by original code. Lighting follows the camera and does not require room lighting; parts are combined by material. No third-party models or textures were added.

## Position the watch

1. Open Settings → Your HA watch → Adjust position & size.
2. Hold the watch hand visibly in front of you. During adjustment, the watch appears even if watch mode was previously off.
3. Use the large − / + buttons to move sideways, up/down, and in depth in 5 mm steps. Directions are relative to the dial. Tilt, side tilt, and rotation change by 5°; size changes by 5 percentage points.
4. Saving the position commits the preview and enables the watch. It then follows the wrist, including rotation; it no longer continuously aligns to the head center.
5. Canceling or closing the menu discards the preview. Resetting the position restores the previous automatic alignment.

Left and right wrists have separate saved settings. Right-/left-handed mode selects the corresponding watch. Wrist offset is limited to 20 cm and scale to 65–160%. Adjustment does not start without a visible watch hand; saving also requires recognized hand tracking. During adjustment, tapping the dial cannot accidentally open another menu.

## Excluding local SDK data

Build inspection found that Meta XR SDK 207 automatically writes local editor/AgentBridge connection data to a Resources asset for development builds. The build now temporarily moves this asset to an editor-only directory after SDK preparation. The packed asset report must confirm it is absent from the APK; otherwise the build fails. The local file is restored afterward, including on build failure. Neither file contents nor credentials are printed or committed to Git. This data belongs to Unity development tooling, not the Home Assistant connection. Earlier development APKs could contain this SDK data; use the corrected build for distribution.
